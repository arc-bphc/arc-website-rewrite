## Linux Dual-Boot Workshop Recap: ARC × CRUx

In collaboration with **CRUx**, we ran a hands-on Linux dual-boot workshop which is the ideal launchpad for ROS. We gave a live walthrough of the entire Dual Boot and WSL Installation process, and helped the Atendees troubleshoot any issues they faced. Many GRUB Menu Rescues happened, Many HP Bootloader issues, many Wrong Disk Partitions were made, But everyone successfully had a Linux Machine in the end, thanks to such talented troubleshooting.

## ROS Workshop Recap

We ran ROS 101 as a **no-install, browser-only** intro to nodes, topics, publishers and subscribers via the [ROS Arcade](https://arc-bphc.netlify.app/ros/). The goal was to get a robot to the green box using the codeblocks provided.

<Image src={ros} alt="ROS workshop" quality={90} style="width:100%; height:auto; border-radius:8px; border:1px solid #353535;" />

*New way to learn fundamentals without dealing with installation headaches*

```bash
# peek at what the robot sees and tell it where to go
ros2 topic echo /scan      # wall sensors
ros2 topic pub /cmd_vel    # velocity commands
```

> You can [check it out yourself](https://arc-bphc.netlify.app/ros/)!

## Our New Induction Process

If the workshops were the trailer, this was the main feature. This year’s induction ran completely through github repos, here is the main one: **[arc-bphc/2026-ARC-Induction](https://github.com/arc-bphc/2026-ARC-Induction#tasks)** and it was built to be brutally honest about what ARC actually does.

**6 tasks, 3 tracks.** Three easy tasks to get your hands dirty and three hard ones that demanded real digging and troubleshooting. You could attempt any task, in any order. The harder tasks ahd not "correct answer", and they focused more on thinking ability. The whole point was breadth: mechanical intuition, embedded, simulation, or code you had to pick your fight, and excel at it.

Every submission came in as a **pull request**. This taught the Candidates version control by default, which is an absolute necessity when working with so many people.

Shortlisted folks were then called for **interviews**. We weren’t hunting for the most polished technical skills. We scored for three things in roughly this order: **enthusiasm, time you can actually give, and competency**.

From that pipeline, we picked **40 of the best candidates for ARC**, and we couldn’t be happier. They’ve walked straight into the builds you saw above, the Robodog, the dual-arm setup, the Amazing Hand and the ATMOS Projects, which are top secret till the ATMOS marketing starts ;).    

> Browse the exact tasks they cracked: **[github.com/arc-bphc/2026-ARC-Induction#tasks](https://github.com/arc-bphc/2026-ARC-Induction#tasks)**

As ARC buckles up for The Most Active ATMOS yet, we expect even better performance from our juniors, and hopefully they shall deliver.
